Calculation definitions

LTV = amount financed ÷ supported vehicle value × 100.

Trade-in equity = trade-in allowance − existing lien payoff. A negative result is negative equity.

Amount-financed reconstruction = cash price + entered financed taxes/fees + GAP + warranty/service contract + other add-ons + other financed charges + negative equity − cash down payment − rebates/credits − positive trade-in equity.

Equal-payment scheduled sum = monthly payment × number of payments.

PTI / DTI and the APR benchmark difference are descriptive only and do not create automatic review items.
