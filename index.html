    Calculation definitions

    Price vs. value = dealer cash price − entered supported vehicle value, with the percentage difference measured against the supported value.

    LTV = amount financed ÷ entered supported vehicle value × 100.

    Down-payment share = cash down payment ÷ dealer cash price × 100.

    Financed add-ons = financed GAP + warranty/service contract + other financed add-ons.

    Total entered financed extras = financed taxes and fees + GAP + warranty/service contract + other financed add-ons + other separately identified financed charges. Rebates and credits are not included in this subtotal.

    APR comparison uses the Q1 2026 Experian average used-vehicle APR for the selected VantageScore 4.0 tier. It is not calculated for a new vehicle, a different/unknown score model, or when APR is not entered.
