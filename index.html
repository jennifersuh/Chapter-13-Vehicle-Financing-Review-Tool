Calculation definitions

LTV = amount financed ÷ entered vehicle value × 100. LTV is shown only when a vehicle value is entered.

Amount-financed reconstruction = cash price + entered financed taxes/fees + GAP + warranty/service contract + other add-ons + other financed charges − cash down payment − rebates/credits.

Projected net monthly income = current net monthly income + entered change in net monthly income.

Projected amount remaining after plan payment = projected net monthly income − current monthly plan payment.

Equal-payment scheduled sum = monthly payment × number of payments.

APR benchmark difference is descriptive only and does not create an automatic review item.
